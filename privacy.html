Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use Online Compiler ("the site").

Your code and files

The files you create are stored only in your own browser (local storage) on your device. We do not upload, read or store your code on our servers. If you clear your browser data, your saved files will be removed, so please download anything important.

Code runs in your browser

Python, HTML, CSS and JavaScript that you run in the editor are executed inside your browser on your device.

Information we do not collect

The site does not ask you to register or log in, and we do not ask for your name, address or phone number.

Third-party services

To work, the site loads open-source tools from third-party content delivery networks, such as the Monaco code editor and the Pyodide Python runtime. When your browser loads these files, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type, as with any website.

Advertising

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also visit www.aboutads.info to opt out of personalised ads from other third-party vendors. For more details, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Cookies and local storage

The site itself uses your browser's local storage to save your files and settings. Advertising partners may place cookies on your device as described above. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use our contact page.
